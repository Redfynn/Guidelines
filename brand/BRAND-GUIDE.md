# RedFynn Brand Guidelines (2024): reference

Source: RedFynn Brand Guidelines 2024, designed by Propaganda (Spokane, WA). Section numbers match the original PDF.

## 1. Brand elements

### 1.1 Logo anatomy and hierarchy
- Three logos: **Primary** (icon, REDFYNN wordmark, TECHNOLOGIES line), **Secondary** (icon and wordmark, no tagline), **Icon** (the circular "R" mark).
- Primary is the first choice. Secondary can be used on its own in places like a website nav bar. The icon is acceptable where the logo needs to be reinforced in a secondary way, such as the bottom of a page.
- The mark is a black circle with a white "R" (which has a crossbar). In the duotone wordmark, "RED" is red and "FYNN" is charcoal. TECHNOLOGIES is small, wide-tracked and slate.

### 1.2 Using the logo
- Clear space: at least the height of the "R" of the wordmark on every side, scaled with the logo.
- Minimum sizes: primary 2 in or 144 px wide. Secondary 1.75 in or 126 px wide. Icon 1/3 in or 24 px wide.

### 1.3 Logo restrictions
Do not: stretch vertically or horizontally, rotate at any angle other than 90 degrees, outline the logo, use any color outside the brand specification, use a gradient on the logo, or separate individual elements of the logo.

## 2. Design system

### 2.1 Color palette
Use exact values for the medium (Pantone, hex or RGB).

| Role | Name | Hex | RGB | CMYK | Pantone |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Charcoal | #1C1B1F | 28 27 31 | 74 69 62 76 | Black 6 C |
| Primary | Red | #ED3E25 | 237 62 37 | 1 91 99 0 | Bright Red C |
| Secondary | White | #FFFFFF | 255 255 255 | 0 0 0 0 | n/a |
| Tertiary | Slate | #4D4C4E | 77 76 78 | 66 59 56 36 | 7540 C |
| Tertiary | Smoke | #D2D1D2 | 210 209 210 | 17 13 13 0 | 427 C |

Rules from the guide:
- Red and Charcoal are used for most color blocks and backgrounds. Shades of the primaries may be used to build layering and hierarchy.
- White is for smaller color blocks, dividers and occasional accents that add contrast to Red.
- Tertiary colors (Slate, Smoke) are shades of Charcoal. Use them only for layering and depth.

### 2.2 Logo color
- Use the duotone logo whenever possible.
- On Charcoal: white-circle duotone (red "RED", white "FYNN"), all-white, or all-red.
- On Red: charcoal-circle duotone (charcoal text), white-circle duotone, or all-charcoal.
- On White: charcoal-circle duotone (red "RED", charcoal "FYNN"), all-charcoal, or all-red.
- Solid white and black versions are only for places where contrast would otherwise be too low.

### 2.3 Type styles
- **TWK Everett** is used almost exclusively. Styles: Light, Regular, Book, Medium, each with italics.
- **Feeling Passionate** (brush script, Regular only) is only an accent for calls to action and taglines.

### 2.4 Type hierarchy
The brand uses size, not weight, for hierarchy and rarely goes above Book.

| Level | Font | Tracking | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overline / eyebrow | TWK Everett Light | wide (100pt tracking), uppercase | small label above a header |
| Header | TWK Everett Book | 0 | very large, tight leading |
| Subheader | TWK Everett Regular | 0 | roughly half the header size |
| Body copy | TWK Everett Regular | 0 | comfortable line height |
| Button | TWK Everett Regular | 0 | pill shape |

Buttons: **Treatment 1** is a filled black pill with white text. **Treatment 2** is a pill with a thin black outline and black text.

### 2.5 Photo treatment
- Noisy orange/red gradients and abstract shapes add texture and visual interest.
- Primary use: background elements for sections such as a website hero or the background of an ad.
- In digital applications, use animated gradients when motion is supported.
- Six sample gradients are in `assets/gradients/` (cropped from the guide).
- Real photography in the ad examples shows smiling small-business owners at work (a baker holding a pizza), placed beside a gradient block. Keep photos warm, candid and human.

## 3. Brand applications (patterns seen in the guide)

- **Out-of-home ads (3.1):** a rounded-corner gradient block with a large black headline at the top-left, a short line and contact line at the bottom ("Redfynn.com | phone"), the logo in a top corner, a photo to the side. Vertical ad: gradient fill, mixed regular and italic copy, small "redfynn.com" at the bottom.
- **Business cards (3.2):** Charcoal front with a large white name, small contact lines at the corners, and a gradient edge strip. The back is a full gradient with the icon in a corner.
- **Stationery (3.3):** white or light grey stock with a thin red-orange line at the bottom edge, charcoal envelopes and folders, gradient panels as accents, the icon or small logo in a corner.
- **Guide page layout:** a thin header rule with a section number, section name and "Brand Guidelines" label, a narrow left text column, and a light grey (#F1F1F1-ish) content panel on the right. Large numbers and headings are light weight.
